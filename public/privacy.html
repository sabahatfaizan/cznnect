Privacy Policy

1. Information collected

Cznnect processes information you provide, including your email address, birthday, profile details, family connections, posts, plans, places, ratings and activity. Authentication credentials are handled by our authentication provider.

2. How information is used

Information helps create and secure your account, connect invited relatives, provide app features, send service notifications, maintain the service and prevent misuse.

3. Family visibility

Your birthday and profile details may be visible to members of the family space you join. Content you add to shared family features is visible to people with access to that family. Only share information you are comfortable sharing with them.

4. Device permissions

Camera access supports QR scanning; location supports location-based features; notifications support reminders and updates. You can manage permissions in your device settings. Some features may not work without them.

5. Email preferences

Essential account and service messages may still be sent. Optional news and promotional emails are separate and can be declined during registration or changed later in Settings, where available.

6. Service providers and security

Cznnect uses third-party infrastructure, including Supabase, for authentication, database and hosting services. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Retention and deletion

Information may be retained while needed to provide the service and for legitimate operational or legal needs. Account and family deletion may depend on the controls available in the app and the permissions of the family host.

8. Younger users

Cznnect is designed for family groups. Younger users should involve a parent or guardian where appropriate. Do not submit another person's personal information unless you have permission to do so.

9. Updates and questions

This policy may change as Cznnect evolves. The current version will be published here. For privacy questions, contact the Cznnect administrator using the contact method provided in the app.
